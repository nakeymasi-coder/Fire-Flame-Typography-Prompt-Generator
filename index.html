✍️ What to Put in the Custom Text Boxes

  • Custom Creative Direction: the mood, style, or overall vibe you want.
  • Must-Have Elements: exact things that must show up in the design.
  • Good examples: royal blue flames, gold metallic edges, glam luxury branding, bold poster look, smoky dark background, spark embers, chrome shine.
  • Bad example: make it cool. That’s vague as hell 😏
Stuck? Go to ChatGPT and type:

“Help me write a custom text box description for a fire typography design for my brand. I want it to feel bold, glam, royal blue, gold, luxury, and high-energy.”
